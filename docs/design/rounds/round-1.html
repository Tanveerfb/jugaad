Signature component: the plan review

The Organiser's moment: a plan of moves you approve before anything touches the disk. Three ways to show it. The file data stays plainly readable in all three.

A Routes

Each move is a route: from, a marching dotted path, a hexagon at the destination. A lever-switch includes it; a mini rotor ring shows confidence. Excluded routes go dark and still.
