Component round 1b

Locked from round 1: tabs as the rotor track, the plan review as destination bays. Here: the console key in three contrast treatments, and three new directions for inputs and selects.

Console key: contrast

Same hexagon key and light; only the primary's colouring changes. Every pair passes WCAG AA in both themes.

A1 Ember

A deeper burnt-orange body with white text. The bright orange moves into the light and the loading sweep.

white on #b33f0f · 5.8 : 1

A2 Lit edge

A navy body with orange text inside a bright orange hexagon edge. Hover fills it with a faint glow.

orange on navy · 6.1 : 1

A3 Split key

An orange end-cap carries the light; the label sits on navy in white. Hover widens the cap and warms the body.

white on navy · 13 : 1

Inputs and selects: new directions

Each shows a text field and a select live, then hover, focus, error, valid and disabled.

I1 Chase frame

The field's edge is the honeycomb tile's edge: on focus a light runs once round it and stays lit.

I2 Key slot

The field is a console key laid flat: its hexagonal end-cap carries the action: clear, open the list, or show the status. Matches the buttons.

I3 Roundel socket

A roundel set into the field's left end is its status light: dim at rest, orange while you type, red on error, aqua when valid.
